# Credit Comeback Club founder video

Target length: 60–90 seconds
Format: horizontal, direct-to-camera, calm delivery

## Script

You are more than a credit score.

I know that because I have been the person getting denied for the car, the apartment, the house, and the loan. I served in the U.S. Air Force, where attention to detail was a standard to live by—but when I was young, I had almost no financial education, and I wrecked my own credit.

When I finally learned how credit reporting and consumer law actually work, I became frustrated by an industry full of gurus, false promises, guarantees, and generic disputes.

That is why I do not think of this as simply sending disputes. At Credit Comeback Club, we build documented cases. We start with the actual three-bureau report. We identify the exact reporting facts, review the personal impact, apply the appropriate consumer-law framework, and keep a clear record of what was sent and what happened next.

I created CCC because everyone deserves a second chance—a comeback—not only in credit, but in life. Fixing my own credit opened doors I thought were permanently closed, and I love helping other people feel that hope again.

I still have not seen a file that felt worse than mine did when I started. You are never too far gone.

Let us look at the report, build a plan, and decide whether CCC is the right fit for your comeback.
